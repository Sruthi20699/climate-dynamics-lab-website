Principal Investigator

Dr. Jane A. Doe

Professor of Atmospheric Science · Director, Climate Dynamics Lab · Fellow, American Geophysical Union

Dr. Doe earned her Ph.D. in Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences from the University of Coastal California and completed postdoctoral research at the National Center for Climate Research. Her work has been supported by NSF, NOAA, and NASA, and she currently serves as an editor for the Journal of Climate.

  • Atmospheric circulation & jet stream dynamics
  • Ocean–atmosphere coupled variability
  • Climate model evaluation & diagnostics
  • Extreme weather & climate predictability
