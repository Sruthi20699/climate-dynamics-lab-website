Earth System & Climate Science Research

Understanding the Physics of a Changing Climate

Large-scale circulation, climate variability, and predictability across timescales

The Climate Dynamics Lab investigates the fundamental physical processes that govern atmospheric and oceanic circulation, and how these processes shape regional and global climate variability. Our work combines theory, numerical modeling, and observational analysis to improve understanding and prediction of the Earth's climate system.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Jane A. Doe

Professor of Atmospheric Science · Director, Climate Dynamics Lab · Fellow, American Geophysical Union

Dr. Doe's research explores the dynamics of large-scale atmospheric circulation and its role in shaping regional climate extremes. Her group uses a hierarchy of models — from idealized dynamical systems to comprehensive Earth system models — to understand mechanisms of climate variability and change. Our Mission

The Climate Dynamics Lab is committed to advancing fundamental understanding of the Earth's climate system through rigorous, physically grounded science — and to translating that understanding into knowledge that supports climate resilience, informed policy, and the next generation of climate scientists.

Advance Fundamental Science

Pursue mechanistic understanding of climate processes across scales, from convection to global circulation.

Train Future Scientists

Mentor students and early-career researchers in quantitative, computational, and observational methods.

Inform Society

Communicate climate science clearly to policymakers, educators, and the public.

Research Themes

Key focus areas driving our group's current work

🌐

Atmospheric Circulation

Investigating the dynamics of jet streams, storm tracks, and planetary waves and their response to a warming climate.

🌊

Ocean–Climate Interaction

Examining how ocean heat transport and variability modes like ENSO influence global and regional climate.

📈

Climate Modeling & Prediction

Developing and evaluating numerical models to improve seasonal-to-decadal climate predictability.

⛈️

Extreme Events

Studying the physical drivers of heatwaves, droughts, and extreme precipitation under climate change.

📡

Observational Diagnostics

Using satellite and reanalysis datasets to diagnose mechanisms of observed climate variability.

🧠

Machine Learning for Climate

Applying data-driven methods to accelerate model emulation and pattern detection in climate data.
